Hello World

October 2026 · 1 min read ·General · Game Design · Undertale
A space for notes on video game design, Undertale, systems programming, and whatever else catches my attention.

Welcome to my corner of the web for longer-form thoughts.

I wanted a simple place to write without the overhead of medium platforms or bloated CMS tools. Moving forward, I plan to write about:

  • Video game design and mechanics: Deconstructing game feel, player agency, narrative friction, and what makes indie games resonate.
  • Undertale: Deep dives into its combat systems, leitmotifs, subversion of RPG tropes, and why its design choices work so effectively.
  • Technical projects & systems: Retrospectives on software architectures, hardware integrations, and experiments that don't fit neatly into a bullet point on a resume.
  • Whatever else catches my interest: Random rabbit holes, tools, or ideas.

Stay tuned for upcoming writeups! If you have thoughts or want to discuss anything, you can reach out via Talk to me.

Thanks for reading. If you have questions, feedback, or want to discuss this, feel free to reach out via the contact form or by email at jjreder@ncsu.edu.
